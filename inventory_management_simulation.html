Inventory Management Simulation

Day-by-day stock simulation using a reorder-point / order-quantity (s, Q) policy.

Simulation settings

Demand is randomly generated using a Poisson distribution. Orders arrive after each product's supplier lead time.

Policy comparison

How it works

  1. Customer demand is generated for each product every day.
  2. Available stock is sold; unmet demand is recorded as lost sales.
  3. If stock plus pending orders is at or below the reorder point, a new order is placed.
  4. The simulation calculates holding, ordering and stock-out costs, service level, revenue and profit.

Total cost = holding cost + ordering cost + stock-out penalty. Profit subtracts purchase cost, holding cost, ordering cost and stock-out penalty from revenue.
